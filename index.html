Authentic and Ethically Sourced Hapé Medicine

When you buy Rapé online from Sacred Snuff, you help support the ancestry and cultural continuity of indigenous peoples in the Amazon. Our Rapé (also known as Hapé or Rapeh) is sold in a fair trade partnership with tribal communities in Brazil, and is rooted in reciprocal sourcing practices that directly reinvest in Amazonian tribes and their land. From the forest to your front door, we maintain the highest sustainability standards to ensure the rainforest continues to thrive, a mission that includes direct biodiversity and reforestation efforts whenever possible.

  • Sourced with reverence for indigenous culture and tradition
  • Help honor and preserve ancestral plant wisdom
  • Products sourced and hand-crafted on Amazonian tribal land
  • Sustainable methods focused on long-term ecological care
  • 100% pure Rapé without additives or fillers

Buy Rapé in the USA that you can feel good about. Rapé and sacred tobacco for sale is as authentic as it gets, so you can experience the true power of Amazonian plant medicine and the ancestral wisdom behind these unique tools for personal and spiritual growth.
