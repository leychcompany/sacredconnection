What is Hapé?

A prayer you receive through the breath.

"Hapé is not taken. It is received — the forest speaking directly to your spirit."

Hapé is a sacred snuff of finely ground mapacho tobacco and ceremonial plants, prepared over days of ritual by indigenous healers in the Amazon basin. Each tribe guards its own recipes, passed from elder to apprentice for centuries.

It is not smoked, and it is not a recreational product. Administered through the nose with a kuripe or tepi pipe, hapé is used in ceremony to ground the body, quiet the mind, clear stagnant energy, and open a deeper state of presence and connection.

Your first ceremony, in three movements

You don't need to be a shaman. You need a quiet moment, an open heart, and respect for the medicine.

I

Set your intention

Find a calm space. Light palo santo if you wish. Ask yourself what you are seeking — grounding, clarity, release — and hold that question gently.

II

Receive the medicine

Using a kuripe (self-applicator), place a pea-sized amount and administer to each nostril with a steady breath. Strong at first — then a wave of stillness.

III

Sit in the silence

Close your eyes for 10–15 minutes. Let the medicine ground you. Many feel a deep clearing of the mind and a quiet, rooted connection to the present.

Good Questions

Before you begin

Is hapé legal in the United States?
Yes. Hapé is a tobacco product and is legal to purchase and possess in the US for adults 21 and over. We ship from our US warehouse to all 50 states, and we verify age at checkout.
I've never used hapé. Which blend should I start with?
We recommend a gentler, grounding blend such as Caboclo Paricá, paired with a bamboo kuripe. Our free First Ceremony Guide walks you through your first experience step by step — or take the blend quiz and we'll match you by intention.
Is this the same as recreational tobacco or snuff?
No. Hapé is a ceremonial preparation used in moments of intention — not habitually. It is made from mapacho (Nicotiana rustica) and sacred plants, ground by hand in ritual. It is never smoked, and we offer tobacco-free blends as well.
How do I know the tribes are treated fairly?
We buy directly from the makers — the people pictured on this page — at prices they set, under long-term partnership agreements. A portion of every sale is reinvested in reforestation and cultural preservation projects on tribal land. We publish our sourcing story openly.
How fresh is the medicine, and how should I store it?
Each batch is sealed at origin and shipped in airtight tins. Stored away from humidity and sunlight, hapé keeps its strength for many months. We rotate small batches, so what reaches you is recent — never warehouse-aged stock.
How fast is shipping?
Orders ship the same business day when placed early. Free standard shipping on orders over $100; expedited options available at checkout.
